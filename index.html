Each point is the absolute difference between consecutive completions (in chronological order), per ticket size. Click a point to exclude the later of the two completions it's based on (or restore it) — the same underlying dataset used by the other tabs.

Upper Range Limit (URL) = 3.27 × average moving range. The lower limit for a moving-range chart is mathematically 0 (ranges can't be negative), so it's shown flat at the bottom. Needs at least 2 points per size.
